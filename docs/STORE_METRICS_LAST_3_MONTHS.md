# Store1920 metrics, last 3 months

26 July 2026 through 26 October 2026

Prepared 26 October 2026 from the Store1920 database. Days run on Asia/Dubai time. Money is AED. This is the public website. Orders typed in by staff are not included.

A sales order is a website order that was placed. Cancelled orders are left out. Failed card, Tabby, and Tamara attempts are left out. Checkouts still waiting for payment are left out. Cash on delivery is included when the customer places the order.

The window starts at midnight on 26 July 2026 and runs through the afternoon of 26 October 2026. The last sales order in this count was placed at 5:11pm Dubai time on 26 October, so that day is not a full day. July in this report is 26–31 July only. August and September are full months.

## Add-to-cart rate

**0.21% of visits have a saved add-to-cart event.**

26 July 2026 through 26 October 2026: 95,244 visits, and 204 of those visits saved an add-to-cart event (889 events).

That rate is too low to be the real shop rate. In the same window, 7,558 visits started checkout (7.94% of visits). A checkout starts from a cart, so more people added a product than the add-to-cart event recorded. The click is only saved on some add-to-cart actions.

## Checkout completion rate

**26.69%** of checkout visits reached a saved purchase event.

7,558 visits started checkout, and 2,017 of those visits have a purchase event.

On the saved-cart list for the same window, **32.12%** of carts were bought: 2,156 of 6,712. The two rates use different records, so they are not the same figure.
